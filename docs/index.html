Markets, research and your portfolios, in one desktop app.

Simcoe Trading puts quotes, charts, company research, rates and macro data, your portfolios and trading through your own broker side by side on your Mac or PC. It's invite-only for now: if you've been invited, download it here.

The latest version, free. What's new

The app isn't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so the first time you open it your computer asks you to confirm. Here's how, step by step. After that it updates itself.

What's inside

Home

Watchlists, charts, market tables and the news, in panels you arrange the way you like and pop out into their own windows.

Research

Any company's profile, financial statements, estimates, ownership, options and technicals, with MODL's ratings and VAL's valuations.

Rates and macro

Yield curves, the odds for each central bank meeting, Treasuries and SOFR, the economic calendar, macro data and a world map.

Portfolios

As many portfolios as you like, from your own transactions, with time- and money-weighted returns in the currency you choose.

Monitor and alerts

Live grids of the names you follow, the day's biggest movers, a daily brief of what changed since yesterday, and alerts when a price or a rating crosses your line.

Trade

An order ticket and an options desk for an Alpaca paper account or your Interactive Brokers account. Orders go from your computer straight to your broker.

Two looks, each dark or light

Evergreen, forest green and cream, is the default. Amber is the other. Switch any time from View › Theme.

Your account

You can use most of Simcoe Trading without one. An account keeps your watchlists, portfolios, alerts and layouts in step between your computers, and once it's approved it opens the analysis: MODL, VAL and the scores in FA, FUND and TECH.

Create it in the app: File › Account. We email you a six-digit code to confirm it. Then tell the person who invited you which email you used, so they can approve it.
